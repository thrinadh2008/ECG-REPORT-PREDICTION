What Is Going On In Your Heart

Pathophysiological Mechanism
Impulses originate from the sinoatrial node located in the superior wall of the right atrium. The electrical depolarization wavefront travels smoothly across the Bachmann bundle and internodal tracts, passing through the atrioventricular node before propagating rapidly down the bundle branches and Purkinje fibers. Blood is pumped efficiently from the atria into the ventricles and forward into systemic circulation.

Essential Health Precautions

Immediate & Daily Care Measures
  • Maintain balanced hydration and electrolytes (potassium, magnesium).
  • Follow the DASH or Mediterranean heart-healthy diet with low saturated fats.
  • Engage in 150 minutes of moderate aerobic cardiovascular activity weekly.
  • Avoid excessive consumption of energy drinks, high caffeine doses, and tobacco.

Disease Insights & Medical Tests

Recommended Next Steps with Doctor
To verify and monitor your cardiac health, consult with a board-certified physician or cardiologist. Ask if the following diagnostic assessments are recommended based on your family history:
  • Transthoracic Echocardiogram (Echo): Evaluates heart chamber dimensions and ejection fraction (EF).
  • 24-48 Hour Holter Monitor: Captures paroxysmal rhythm anomalies during daily routines.
  • High-Sensitivity Cardiac Troponin-I: Biomarker to rule out subclinical myocardial ischemia.
  • Lipid & Metabolic Panel: Assesses LDL, HDL, triglycerides, and fasting glucose levels.
