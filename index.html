4. Detailed Pathophysiological Mechanism (Inside the Heart)
Electrical impulses originate rhythmically from the Sinoatrial (SA) node in the high right atrium. Electrical depolarization sweeps through the atrial syncytium, traverses the AV node with physiological delay, then rapidly propagates down the His-Purkinje network, achieving synchronous ventricular ejection.
